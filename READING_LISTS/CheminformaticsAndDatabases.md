# Cheminformatics & Databases Reading List

This reading list focuses on the core database products of our lab (MolMeDB, ChannelsDB, MOLEonline) along with fundamental concepts in cheminformatics, FAIR data, and ELIXIR research data management.

---

## 1. Local Lab Database Literature

### MolMeDB (Molecules on Membranes Database)
* **The MolMeDB Publication:** Juračka, J., Šrejber, M., Melíková, M., Bazgier, V., & Berka, K. (2019). *MolMeDB: Molecules on Membranes Database.* Database, 2019, baz078. [https://doi.org/10.1093/database/baz078](https://doi.org/10.1093/database/baz078)
* *Note:* Review this paper to understand how partitioning coefficients ($K_{ow}$) and penetration profiles are calculated, indexed, and stored in the database.

### MOLEonline & ChannelsDB (Protein Tunnels and Channels)
* **MOLEonline Web Server:** Sehnal, D., et al. (2013). *MOLEonline 2.0: web portal for analysis of macromolecular channels.* Nucleic Acids Research, 41(W1), W398-W404. [https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkt378](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkt378)
* **ChannelsDB Database:** Pravda, L., et al. (2018). *ChannelsDB: database of biomacromolecular tunnels and channels.* Nucleic Acids Research, 46(D1), D399-D405. [https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkx868](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkx868)

## 2. Cheminformatics Basics

### Representations & Toolkits
* **SMILES Specification:** Weininger, D. (1988). *SMILES, a chemical language and information system. 1. Introduction to methodology and encoding rules.* Journal of Chemical Information and Computer Sciences, 28(1), 31-36. [https://doi.org/10.1021/ci00057a005](https://doi.org/10.1021/ci00057a005)
* **RDKit Python Toolkit:** *Getting Started with the RDKit in Python.* [https://www.rdkit.org/docs/GettingStartedInPython.html](https://www.rdkit.org/docs/GettingStartedInPython.html) (Essential for handling chemical structures programmatically).

---

## 3. FAIR Data & Research Data Management

### Guidelines
* **The FAIR Principles:** Wilkinson, M. D., et al. (2016). *The FAIR Guiding Principles for scientific data management and stewardship.* Scientific Data, 3(1), 1-9. [https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.18](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.18)
* **ELIXIR RDMkit:** The Research Data Management Toolkit for Life Sciences. [https://rdmkit.elixir-europe.org/](https://rdmkit.elixir-europe.org/) (Co-contributed to by Karel Berka, detailing data life cycles).
* **WorkflowHub:** A registry for scientific workflows. [https://workflowhub.eu/](https://workflowhub.eu/) (Search for Chemical Data Lab workflows).
