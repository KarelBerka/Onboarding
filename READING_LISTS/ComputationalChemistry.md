# Computational Chemistry Reading List

This reading list contains essential literature, textbooks, and online tutorials to help you understand Molecular Dynamics (MD) simulations, molecular docking, and structural visualization.

---

## 1. Molecular Dynamics (MD) Simulations

### Core Concepts & Force Fields
* **Book:** *Understanding Molecular Simulation: From Algorithms to Applications* by Daan Frenkel and Berend Smit. (The bible of molecular simulations).
* **Review Paper:** Adcock, S. A., & McCammon, J. A. (2006). *Molecular dynamics: survey of methods for simulating macromolecules.* Chemical Reviews, 106(5), 1589-1615. [https://doi.org/10.1021/cr040426m](https://doi.org/10.1021/cr040426m)
* **GROMACS Tutorial:** Justin Lemkul's GROMACS tutorials are the standard starting point.
  * *Lysozyme in Water:* [http://www.mdtutorials.com/gmx/lysozyme/index.html](http://www.mdtutorials.com/gmx/lysozyme/index.html)
  * *Membrane Protein (KALP15 in DPPC):* [http://www.mdtutorials.com/gmx/membrane_protein/index.html](http://www.mdtutorials.com/gmx/membrane_protein/index.html) (Highly relevant for membrane work!).

---

## 2. Molecular Docking & Virtual Screening

### Principles & Software
* **AutoDock Vina:** Trott, O., & Olson, A. J. (2010). *AutoDock Vina: improving the speed and accuracy of docking with a new scoring function, efficient optimization, and multithreading.* Journal of Computational Chemistry, 31(2), 455-461. [https://doi.org/10.1002/jcc.21334](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcc.21334)
* **Virtual Screening Review:** Pyzer-Knapp, E. O., et al. (2015). *Learning to play hide and seek: how machine learning can improve virtual screening.* Annual Review of Physical Chemistry, 66, 549-568. [https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-physchem-040214-121615](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-physchem-040214-121615)

---

## 3. Visualization Tools

### PyMOL & VMD
* **PyMOL Wiki:** [pymolwiki.org](https://pymolwiki.org) – The best reference for commands, scripts, and figure making.
* **VMD (Visual Molecular Dynamics) Tutorial:** UIuc theoretical biophysics group tutorials for VMD and molecular graphics. [http://www.ks.uiuc.edu/Training/Tutorials/vmd/](http://www.ks.uiuc.edu/Training/Tutorials/vmd/)
